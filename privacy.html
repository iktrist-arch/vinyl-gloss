Vinyl Gloss Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

The short version: Vinyl Gloss doesn't collect, store, sell or share any of your personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your music stays between your Mac and Apple Music.

Who we are

Vinyl Gloss is a Mac app that plays your Apple Music library as a record player. It is made by Tristan R ("we", "us").

Information we collect

None. We don't run servers for Vinyl Gloss, and the app never sends information about you or your listening to us or to anyone else.

Apple Music

With your permission, Vinyl Gloss uses Apple's MusicKit to:

  • show the albums, playlists and songs in your Apple Music library,
  • look up full albums and songs in the Apple Music catalog,
  • play music, and
  • add or remove songs from your Apple Music Favorites when you tap a heart.

All of this happens directly between your Mac and Apple. We never see your library, what you play or your Apple account. Apple's handling of this information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. You can turn off Vinyl Gloss's access at any time in System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Media & Apple Music.

Information stored on your Mac

Vinyl Gloss saves a few settings on your Mac so the app works the way you left it: your sort order, whether you were browsing albums or playlists, and the song and spot you were listening to. These stay on your Mac, are never sent anywhere, and are removed if you delete the app.

Subscriptions and payments

Subscriptions are bought and managed through Apple. We never receive your name, email, card details or other payment information. The app only checks with Apple whether your subscription is active. You can manage or cancel your subscription in the App Store under your account settings.

Volume control

The volume slider changes your Mac's output volume, the same as the volume keys. No audio is recorded and no microphone is used.

Children

Vinyl Gloss doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
