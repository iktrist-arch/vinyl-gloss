Vinyl Gloss Support

Your Apple Music library, played like records.

Need help? Email ik.trist@icloud.com and you'll get a reply as soon as possible. Include your Mac model and macOS version if something isn't working.

What you need

  • A Mac with macOS 14 or later.
  • An active Apple Music subscription, signed in to the Music app.

Common questions

The app says it needs access to Apple Music.
Open System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Media & Apple Music, turn on Vinyl Gloss, then reopen the app.

Nothing plays.
Check that your Apple Music subscription is active and that you're signed in to the Music app with the same Apple Account. Music streams from Apple Music, so you also need an internet connection.

An album only shows some of its songs.
Vinyl Gloss looks up the full album on Apple Music. If you're offline it shows the songs saved in your library, and fills in the rest once you're back online.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Open the App Store, click your name, then Account Settings ▸ Subscriptions ▸ Manage. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid the next charge.

I already subscribed on another Mac.
Click Restore Purchases on the subscribe screen, using the same Apple Account.

Do hearts sync with Apple Music?
Yes. A heart in Vinyl Gloss is the same as Favorite in the Music app and on your iPhone.
